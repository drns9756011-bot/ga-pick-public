# Customer Interface

Updated 2026-10-25. Applies to home, quote submission, quote lookup, brand packages, subscription, shopping and privacy. No hosting migration to Wix.

## References And Evidence

- Wix Electronics Store (Modern): https://www.wix.com/website-template/view/html/4888 . Inspected the returned screenshot: large product photography, restrained navigation and unframed visual sections.
- Wix Furniture Manufacturer (Modern): https://www.wix.com/website-template/view/html/4733 . Inspected the returned screenshot: generous typography, clear section hierarchy and large photographs.
- Wix Online Furniture Store (Fresh): https://www.wix.com/website-template/view/html/5223 . Read the preview structure; no claim of inspecting every template or every responsive state.
- Scraped branding on the preview pages identifies Wix's surrounding viewer rather than reliably identifying each template. Those colors and type measurements were not copied.

## Implementation Choices

Palette is the user's existing brand navy #123b67, ink #142c43, muted #5b6c7d, white #ffffff, surface #f4f6f8 and line #dbe3eb. These are chosen application tokens, not measurements extracted from Wix. SUIT Variable is served locally. Body is 17px; page headings are 30-44px; the brand display is larger only on the home hero. All tracking is zero.

Keep navigation consistent, preserve square category tiles, use one focused quote form and disclose its image preview only when an actual image is attached in the quote-present path. Product selection and recommendation precede phone verification; verification remains mandatory before registration. The product catalog uses a desktop category rail and a mobile horizontal category menu.
